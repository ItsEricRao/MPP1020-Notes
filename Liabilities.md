## Definition
> A present obligation of the entity to transfer an economic resource as a result of past events.

## Three Criteria of Definition
1. **Present obligation:** A duty to act or perform in a certain way in the future.
2. **Transfer of economic resources:** Could involve cash settlement, or the provision of goods or services.
3. **Past event:** The event must have already occurred; the intention to do something in the future is not adequate.

## Current vs. Non-Current Liabilities
Liabilities are generally classified as **current** or **non-current** according to the timing of the payment.
- **Current liabilities:** Obligations expected to be settled within one year (12 months). Critical for managing short-term financial obligations and liquidity.
- **Non-current liabilities:** Obligations expected to be settled beyond one year (12 months). Essential for long-term financial planning and stability.

## Examples
- **Current:** Accounts Payable, Overdraft, Credit Card
- **Non-current:** Bank Loan (if repayable beyond 12 months)
