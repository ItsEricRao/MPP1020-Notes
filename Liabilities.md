## Definition
A present obligation of the entity to transfer an economic resource as a result of past events.

## Three Criteria of Definition
- **Present Obligation:** Present obligation means a duty to act or perform in a certain way in the future.
- **Transfer of Economic Resources:** Transfer of an economic resource could involve cash settlement, or the provision of goods or services.
- **Past Event:** A past event must have already occurred; the intention to do something in future is not adequate.

## Current vs. Non-Current Liabilities
- Liabilities are also generally classified into **current** and **non-current** liabilities according to the timing of the payment.
- **Current Liabilities:** Current liabilities are obligations expected to be settled within one year (12 months). Current liabilities are critical for managing short-term financial obligations and liquidity.
- **Non-Current Liabilities:** Obligations expected to be settled beyond one year (12 months) are known as non-current liabilities. Non-current liabilities are essential for long-term financial planning and stability.
