## Definition
> Decreases in [[Assets|assets]], or increases in [[Liabilities|liabilities]], that result in decreases in [[Equity|equity]], other than those relating to distributions to holders of equity claims.

## Three Criteria of Definition
1. Decrease in assets or increase in liabilities
2. Leading to a decrease in equity
3. For reasons other than distributions to the owner

## Examples
- Wages
- Rent
- Electricity
- Insurance

## Debit and Credit
Increased by a **debit**, decreased by a **credit**. Normal balance: debit. See [[Debit and Credit]].
