## Definition
A service business provides intangible products (services) rather than physical goods.

## Types of Service Businesses
- **Professional services:** legal, accounting, consulting
- **Personal services:** hairdressing, fitness training, coaching
- **Maintenance services:** cleaning, repairs, IT support
- **Hospitality & tourism:** hotels, travel agencies, restaurants
- **Education & training:** schools, online courses, tutoring
