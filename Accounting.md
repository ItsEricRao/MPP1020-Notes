## Definition
Accounting is the process of recording and categorizing financial transactions related to a business. The accounting process includes summarizing, analyzing, and [[Report|reporting]] these transactions to oversight agencies, [[Authorities|regulators]], and tax collection entities. The financial statements used in accounting are a concise summary of financial transactions over an accounting period, summarizing a company's operations, financial position, and cash flows.

Must include two parties.

Accounting involves identifying, measuring, recording, and communicating information to [[User|users]] to help them make decisions.
- Identifying transactions and events that relate to an entity
- Measuring or quantifying them in monetary terms
- Recording them in the accounting system
- Communicating the information in a meaningful format to users
