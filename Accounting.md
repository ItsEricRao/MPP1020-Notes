## Definition
Accounting is the process of recording and categorizing financial [[Transaction|transactions]] related to a business. The accounting process includes summarizing, analyzing, and [[Report|reporting]] these transactions to oversight agencies, [[Authorities|regulators]], and tax collection entities. The financial statements used in accounting are a concise summary of financial transactions over an accounting period, summarizing a company's operations, financial position, and cash flows.

## Accounting Process
Accounting involves identifying, measuring, recording, and communicating information to [[User|users]] to help them make decisions.
1. **Identifying** transactions and events that relate to an entity
2. **Measuring** (quantifying) them in monetary terms
3. **Recording** them in the accounting system
4. **Communicating** the information in a meaningful format to users
