## Definition
A **trial balance** is a list of [[General Ledger|ledger]] accounts and their balances on a specific date.
- It is prepared periodically, before the financial statements are prepared. It helps verify that the double-entry recording process has been completed properly, and helps detect errors.
- The total of the debit balances should equal the total of the credit balances. See [[Debit and Credit#Double-Entry Rule]].
- It is step 6 of the [[Accounting Cycle|accounting cycle]]: after the [[Balance Day Adjustments|balance day adjustments]], and before the [[Report#Financial Statements|financial statements]] are prepared.

## Steps
1. List the ledger account names and their closing balances. See [[General Ledger#Balancing (Closing) Ledger Accounts]].
2. Total the debit and credit columns.
3. Make sure the total of the debits equals the total of the credits.

Each closing balance goes in the column for the side it is on:
- **Debit column:** accounts with a debit balance. These are usually [[Assets|assets]], [[Expense|expenses]] and drawings.
- **Credit column:** accounts with a credit balance. These are usually [[Liabilities|liabilities]], capital, [[Income|income]] and contra-assets such as [[Depreciation|accumulated depreciation]].

## Format
**Trial Balance of Business Name**
**As at …**

| Drawings | Amount | |
| Income account | | Amount |
| Expense account | Amount | |
| **Total** | **Total Dr** | **Total Cr** |

## From Trial Balance to Financial Statements
The trial balance is the starting point for the financial statements. Each account is classified as A, L, OE, I or E, and each class goes into a particular statement. See [[Report#Preparing Financial Statements]].
