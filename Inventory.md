## Definition
> Inventories are assets:
> - held for sale in the ordinary course of business;
> - in the process of production for such sale; or
> - in the form of materials or supplies to be consumed in the production process or in the rendering of services.

This definition comes from [[GENERALLY ACCEPTED ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLES (GAAP)#Accounting Standards|AASB 102 Inventories]].

- Inventory is also known as **stock** or **merchandise**.
- Inventory is a **current [[Assets|asset]]**, recorded in the [[Balance Sheet|balance sheet]].
- Selling inventory is the primary source of revenue for a [[Trading Business|trading business]].

## Accounting for Inventory
There are two methods to account for inventory:
1. **[[Perpetual Inventory System]]:** initially records inventory as an asset (Inventory).
2. **Periodic Inventory System:** initially records inventory as an expense (Purchases).

MPP1020 uses only the perpetual inventory system.

The recorded inventory balance is checked against the physical inventory on hand with a [[Stocktake|stocktake]].

## Debit and Credit
Inventory is an asset: increased by a **debit**, decreased by a **credit**. Normal balance: debit. See [[Debit and Credit]].
