## Internal Users
- Include directors, managers, senior executives, supervisors: those with a position of power.
- They are considered "internal" as they have access to confidential information within the entity, as it is essential to running the business.
- Many of their decisions relate to managing the business on a day-to-day basis, relying on both financial accounting and management accounting data.
- They need to respond to risks and opportunities as they occur, so they always need to be well informed.

## External Users
- Users who are not in a privileged position to access information must wait for the official release of [[Report|financial statements]].
- The information they receive is limited by the very summarized and general content provided within these statements.
- Since there are a wide range of user types within this category, they naturally have different information needs. General purpose financial statements available to them are intended to satisfy their common information needs.

## Primary Users
- Those who provide resources to an entity, and require information to make decisions about the resources they provide.
- Financial reporting is **primarily directed** at these users.
- They include:
	- **Investors:** shareholders, both existing and potential
	- **Lenders:** banks and other financial institutions
	- **Other creditors:** employees, suppliers, and customers

## Other Users
- Government and [[Authorities|regulatory agencies]]
- Members of the public
- Suppliers, customers and employees when they are not resource providers

Financial reporting is **not directed at these users**, but they can utilize the information for decision making.
