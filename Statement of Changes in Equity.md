## Definition
The **statement of changes in equity** is a financial report that outlines the movement in an entity's [[Equity|equity]] over a specific accounting period.
- It includes **only the equity accounts** (capital, additional capital contributions and drawings). It also includes the **profit (loss)** from the [[Income Statement|income statement]], and the **balance of capital at the end of the last period**.
- It helps [[User|users]] understand how profits, losses, dividends and other equity-related transactions affect the entity's overall financial position.

## Template
**Name of the Business**
**Statement of Changes in Equity**
**For the period ending …**

| | $ |
| --- | --: |
| Capital at the start of the period | Amount |
| Add: Additional capital contribution | Amount |
| Add: Profit / (Loss) | Amount |
| Less: Drawings | (Amount) |
| **Capital at the end of the period** | Amount |

$$
\text{Closing Capital} = \text{Opening Capital} + \text{Additional Capital} + \text{Profit (Loss)} - \text{Drawings}
$$
A loss reduces capital, so it is subtracted instead of added.

## Link to the Other Statements
- **In:** the profit (loss) comes from the [[Income Statement|income statement]].
- **Out:** the capital at the end of the period goes to the equity section of the [[Balance Sheet|balance sheet]].

See [[Report#Link Between the Financial Statements]].
