## Definition
Debit and credit describe the two sides of an account, and how a [[Transaction|transaction]] is recorded in the accounting system.
- **Debit (Dr):** the left side
- **Credit (Cr):** the right side

## Double-Entry Rule
Every transaction affects at least two accounts. **Total debits must always equal total credits**, which keeps the [[Accounting#Accounting Equation|accounting equation]] in balance.

## Rules of Debit and Credit

Move Expense to the left side of the equation. Elements on the **left** increase with a **Dr** (left side). Elements on the **right** increase with a **Cr** (right side).
$$
\underbrace{\text{Assets} + \text{Expense}}_{\text{Dr}\,\uparrow \quad \text{Cr}\,\downarrow}
\;=\;
\underbrace{\text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity} + \text{Income}}_{\text{Cr}\,\uparrow \quad \text{Dr}\,\downarrow}
$$

| Element | Debit (Dr) | Credit (Cr) | Normal balance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Assets]] | Increase | Decrease | Debit |
| [[Expense\|Expenses]] | Increase | Decrease | Debit |
| [[Equity]] | Decrease | Increase | Credit |
| [[Income]] (Revenue) | Decrease | Increase | Credit |
| [[Liabilities]] | Decrease | Increase | Credit |

- **Assets & Expenses:** increase with a debit, decrease with a credit.
- **Liabilities, Equity & Income:** increase with a credit, decrease with a debit.
- The **normal balance** of an account is the side it increases on.
