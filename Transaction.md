To be a business transaction, the transaction must be related to the business, and it may be either on a **Cash basis** or an **Accrual basis (credit)**.

## Cash
The **cash basis** transaction involves an exchange of money immediately.
- **For example**: A business purchases a new computer for $1,200 and pays by cash, or provides a service and receives $500 cash from the customer.

## Accrual
The **accrual basis (credit)** transaction means a business event has occurred that needs recording, but it does not affect the business bank account at that time. That means cash was neither received nor paid, but will involve a cash flow (an inflow or an outflow) at a later time.
- **For example**: A business purchases office stationery on credit for $300 from a supplier, or the business invoices a customer for a $400 service provided.
### Credit Terms
**“CREDIT TERMS”** Conditions under which a seller is willing to allow the buyer to purchase goods or services on credit. These terms specify:
- Payment due date
- Discounts for early payment
- Interest or penalties for late payment.
