## Definition
To be a business transaction, the transaction must:
- Be related to the business
- Involve two parties

A transaction is either on a **cash basis** or an **accrual (credit) basis**.

## Cash Basis
A **cash basis** transaction involves an immediate exchange of money.

**Examples**
- A business purchases a new computer for $1,200 and pays in cash.
- A business provides a service and receives $500 cash from the customer.

## Accrual Basis (Credit)
An **accrual basis (credit)** transaction is a business event that needs recording but does not affect the business bank account at that time. Cash is neither received nor paid yet, but there will be a cash flow (an inflow or an outflow) at a later time.

**Examples**
- A business purchases office stationery on credit for $300 from a supplier.
- A business invoices a customer for a $400 service provided.

### Credit Terms
**Credit terms** are the conditions under which a seller is willing to allow the buyer to purchase goods or services on credit. These terms specify:
- Payment due date
- Discounts for early payment
- Interest or penalties for late payment
