## Definition
> The residual interest in the [[Assets|assets]] of the entity after deducting all its [[Liabilities|liabilities]].

$$
\text{Equity} = \text{Assets} - \text{Liabilities}
$$

- Equity is dependent on the definitions and measurement of assets and liabilities.
- Equity can also be thought of as **owner's equity (OE)**: the owner's investment in the business.

## Examples
- Capital
- Drawings (reduce equity)
