## Definition
> The residual interest in the [[Assets|assets]] of the entity after deducting all its [[Liabilities|liabilities]].

$$
\text{Equity} = \text{Assets} - \text{Liabilities}
$$

- Equity is dependent on the definitions and measurement of assets and liabilities.
- Equity can also be thought of as **owner's equity (OE)**: the owner's investment in the business.

## Examples
- Capital (including additional capital contributions)
- Retained Earnings
- Profit (increases equity), from the [[Income Statement|income statement]]
- Drawings (reduce equity)

## Reporting
The movement in equity over the period is shown in the [[Statement of Changes in Equity|statement of changes in equity]]. The closing capital is then reported in the [[Balance Sheet|balance sheet]].

## Debit and Credit
Increased by a **credit**, decreased by a **debit**. Normal balance: credit. See [[Debit and Credit]].
- Drawings reduce equity, so an increase in drawings is recorded as a **debit**.
