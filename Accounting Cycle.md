## Definition
The accounting cycle is the sequence of steps that turns business [[Transaction|transactions]] into financial [[Report|reports]] for [[User|users]].

![[Accounting Cycle.png]]

## Steps
1. **Identify** transactions from source (primary) documents.
2. **Analyse** each transaction for its effects on accounts. See [[Transaction#Analysis]].
3. **Record** transaction details in the [[General Journal]] (GJ).
4. **Post** general journal entries to [[General Ledger]] (GL) accounts.
5. **Balance day adjustments.**
6. Prepare a **Trial Balance** of general ledger account balances.
7. Prepare the **financial reports (statements)** from the trial balance. See [[Report#Financial Statements]].
