## Definition
A **ledger** is a book (or collection) of accounts in which account transactions are recorded.
- It keeps all changes (increases and decreases) to each account balance in one place.
- The changes come from the entries recorded in the [[General Journal]].
- It gives management the balance of each account.

## Forms of Ledger Account
1. **T-account form** (MPP1020 uses only this form)
2. **Account (continuous balance) form**

### T-Account
**Account Name**

| Date | Details | Dr | Date | Details | Cr |
| --- | --- | --: | --- | --- | --: |
| | | | | | |

The left side is the **debit** side and the right side is the **credit** side. See [[Debit and Credit]].

## Posting
**Posting** is transferring the information from journal entries to the ledger accounts.
- Post in chronological (date) order: post all the debits and credits of one journal entry before moving to the next entry.
- For the account **debited**: on its debit side, record the date, the details (name of the account credited) and the debit amount.
- For the account **credited**: on its credit side, record the date, the details (name of the account debited) and the credit amount.

## Balancing (Closing) Ledger Accounts
Accounts are balanced at the end of each financial reporting period:
1. Total both sides of the account, and write the larger total on both sides.
2. On the side with the smaller total, enter the difference as the **closing balance**.
3. Carry the balancing figure down to the opposite side as the **opening balance** for the next period.
