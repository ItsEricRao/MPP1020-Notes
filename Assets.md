## Definition
> A present economic resource controlled by the entity as a result of past events. An economic resource is a right that has the potential to produce economic benefits.

## Three Criteria of Definition
1. **Right:** Rights can involve an obligation of another party (e.g. to receive cash, goods or services), and can also represent rights over assets.
2. **Control:** Control often equates to ownership.
3. **Potential economic benefit inflow:** The potential to produce economic benefits need not be certain.

## Current vs. Non-Current Assets
Assets are generally classified as **current** or **non-current** according to their liquidity.
- **Current assets:** Expected to be converted into cash, sold, or consumed within one year (12 months). Crucial for managing day-to-day operations and ensuring liquidity.
- **Non-current assets:** Expected to provide economic benefits beyond one year (12 months). Essential for long-term growth and stability.

## Examples
- **Current:** Cash, Accounts Receivable, Inventory
- **Non-current:** Equipment, Buildings
