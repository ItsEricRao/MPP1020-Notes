## Definition
The **cost of sales** is the total cost of [[Inventory|inventory]] sold during the period. It is also called the **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**.
- It is an [[Expense|expense]] directly related to the revenue recognised from the sale of the goods.
- It is measured at the **cost price** of the goods sold, not the selling price.
- Sales revenue less cost of sales is **gross profit**. See [[Trading Business#Revenue Measurement Process]].

## Formula
$$
\text{COGS} = \text{Beginning Inventory} + \text{Purchases} - \text{Ending Inventory}
$$
Purchases are the purchases made during the period, less any purchase returns.

## Under the Perpetual Inventory System
Under the [[Perpetual Inventory System|perpetual inventory system]], COGS is recorded at the point of each sale, so it is available at any time during the financial year.

## Debit and Credit
Cost of sales is an expense: increased by a **debit**, decreased by a **credit**. Normal balance: debit. See [[Debit and Credit]].
